Welcome to the EASyWEB FORK!
What is EASyWEB you may ask?
EASyWEB is a Trilithic EASyPLUS simulator designed for anyone that doesn't have access to a real unit.

So... what can EASyWEB do?
EASyWEB is designed as an EAS decoder, not a full ENDEC. Technically, you can encode alerts but you have to actually get a SAME header. It has a character generator (CGEN) similar to the EASyPLUS, including all of its scroll positions and color options. Oh yea, you can apply a motion blur to it too. It has distorted audio just like the real EASyPLUS that took off the shelf parts. That's pretty much it, a fancier WEBDEC.
